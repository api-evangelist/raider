This quick boss guide covers every boss in Temple of Sethraliss, including Adderis and Aspix, Merektha, Galvazzt, and Avatar of Sethraliss. Whether you’re tanking, healing, or DPSing, this guide explains key mechanics, dangerous abilities, interrupts, and any positioning requirements necessary...
