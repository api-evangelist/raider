Kings’ Rest is an instance that made its first appearance in World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth, and is now one of the dungeons to return to the Mythic+ lineup in World of Warcraft: Midnight, Season 2. This quick boss guide covers every boss in Kings’ Rest, including The Golden Serpent, Mchimba the Embalmer, The Council of Tribes, and Dazar, the First King. Whether you’re tanking, healing, or DPSing, this guide explains key mechanics, dangerous abilities, interrupts, and any positioning requirements...
